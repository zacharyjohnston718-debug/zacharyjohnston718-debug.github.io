Projects

Three of these are marked on the car above.

  1. FSAE Suspension App

    A MATLAB app linking suspension geometry, coilover hardware, mass, tires, anti-roll bars and component loads in one car model, replacing the team's paper calculations. It grew from my ANSYS Motion model, drove the design of eight suspension components on this year's car (three of them mine), and supplies ride-height and mass data to the aero, frame, brake and traction-control work.

    • MATLAB
    • Vehicle dynamics
    • Engineering software
    On the car
  2. Image

    Suspension component design

    The front and rear shock towers and the rear rockers for this year's car, designed from my Suspension App's results: modeled in Fusion 360, checked with FEA in ANSYS Mechanical, and designed for additive manufacturing.

    • Fusion 360
    • ANSYS Mechanical
    • Additive manufacturing
  3. Image

    Tire data analysis

    Magic Formula tire models fitted to test data for lateral and longitudinal force, aligning moment and combined slip, within 2.6–3.0% RMSE of each load's peak on runs kept out of the fit. Now the Suspension App's tire model.

    • Tire modeling
    • Pacejka Magic Formula
    • MATLAB

Other work

  • Pulse oximeter teardown and redesign

    Freshman design course, team project

    We took apart a commercial pulse oximeter, presented how each component works, and redesigned it in SolidWorks. I modeled the circuit boards and the outer shells.

  • Arduino whack-a-mole game

    Freshman design course, team leader

    Led a four-person team to build a reaction game within a set component budget. I designed the circuit (daisy-chained shift registers driving four RGB LEDs and reading four buttons), wrote the control code, and designed several of the 3D-printed parts.

  • Running gait biomechanics research

    University at Albany research program, high school, three years

    Compared how different running shoes change gait in 18 track athletes at two running speeds, using a linear mixed-effects model to test significance. I wrote the paper, which was reviewed by a board, and presented the findings at [symposium name].
